
# Research and Scholarly Interpretations

This directory stores scholarly research,
interpretations, comparisons and unresolved questions
concerning Somali poetic metrics.

## Research Principles

1. Gaarriye's original rules remain the primary authority
   for this project's strict analysis engine.
2. Scholarly interpretations must be recorded separately.
3. Conflicting interpretations must remain visible.
4. AI-generated proposals are not authoritative evidence.
5. No research claim becomes a Gaarriye rule
   without direct source verification.

## Claim Status

- SUPPORTED
- PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED
- DISPUTED
- UNVERIFIED
- REJECTED

## Research Record

Each research claim must contain:

- Claim ID
- Researcher
- Publication title
- Publication year
- Page reference
- Original quotation or evidence
- Interpretation
- Related Gaarriye rule
- Verification status
- Review history

## Comparison Topics

- Moraic approaches to Somali metrics
- Syllabic approaches
- Vowel quantity
- Consonant behavior
- Phonetic grouping
- Metrical segmentation
- Genre classification
- Teachability and historical interpretations

## Conflict Policy

Conflicts must be documented, reviewed and resolved
through an explicit decision record.

AI must never silently resolve a scholarly conflict.

## Activation gate
A rule must not be activated in an analysis mode until its class, source, exact evidence, conditions, exceptions, and review decision are checked. `PROPOSED`, `UNVERIFIED`, `NEEDS_REVIEW`, and `CONFLICTING` rules are not executable.
